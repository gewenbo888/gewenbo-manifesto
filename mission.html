One Mission一 使 命

Use science, technology, and engineering to solve real problems, meet people's needs, and create new possibilities and trends. 用科学、技术和工程，解决真实的问题，满足人们的需求，创造新的可能和趋势。

§ 01.1 · The Mission, Stated

A mission is not a wish. It is a direction held long enough to bend a life around it — a standing answer to the question "what is worth my effort?" 使命不是愿望。它是一种被坚持得足够久、足以让一生围绕其弯折的方向——是对"什么值得我投入"这一问题的长久回答。

This one is deliberately plain. Not to dominate, not to be remembered, not to win — but to take the most reliable tools humanity has ever built (science to understand, technology to extend, engineering to make real) and aim them at problems that actually press on people's lives. 这条使命刻意保持朴素。不是为了支配，不是为了被铭记，不是为了取胜——而是拿起人类建造过的最可靠的工具（用科学去理解、用技术去延展、用工程去落地），把它们对准真正压在人们生活上的问题。

Everything else in this Psyverse — every identity, principle, domain, and vision that follows — is downstream of this single sentence. 这片心域中的其余一切——随后展开的每一重身份、原则、领域与愿景——都是这一句话的下游。

§ 01.2 · Three Verbs

What the mission actually asks使命真正要求的三个动词

01 · Solve
Solve real problems解决真实的问题
"Real" is the load-bearing word. Not problems invented to sell a solution — problems that would still hurt if no one were watching. Health, energy, knowledge, coordination, loneliness. "真实"是承重的词。不是为了兜售方案而虚构的问题——而是即便无人注视也依然作痛的问题：健康、能源、知识、协作、孤独。
02 · Meet
Meet human needs满足人们的需求
A solution no one can reach is a paper. Meeting a need means delivery, affordability, dignity — the unglamorous last mile where most good ideas quietly die. 无人能触及的方案只是一篇论文。满足需求意味着送达、可负担、有尊严——正是这最不光鲜的"最后一公里"，让大多数好想法悄然夭折。
03 · Create
Create possibilities & trends创造新的可能与趋势
Beyond fixing what's broken: open doors that did not exist. A new capability becomes a new expectation becomes a new normal. That cascade is how the future is actually made. 超越修补已坏之物：推开本不存在的门。一种新能力变成一种新期待，再变成一种新常态。正是这道级联，真正造出了未来。
§ 01.3 · The Instrument

Science · Technology · Engineering科学 · 技术 · 工程

Three faculties, one chain. They are not interchangeable — each fails without the others. 三种官能，一条链。它们不可互换——彼此缺席便各自失效。

Science→Technology→Engineering

Science asks what is true and gives us models that predict. Technology turns a true model into a capability — a lever the hand can pull. Engineering turns a capability into something dependable, affordable, and shipped, under real constraints of time, cost, and matter. 科学追问何为真，给出能够预测的模型。技术把真模型变成一种能力——一根手可拉动的杠杆。工程则在时间、成本与物质的真实约束下，把能力变成可靠、可负担、可交付之物。

Understanding without building is commentary. Building without understanding is luck. The mission insists on both, in order. 只理解而不建造，是评论；只建造而不理解，是侥幸。使命坚持二者兼备，并讲求次序。

"The best way to predict the future is to build the parts of it that ought to exist." "预测未来最好的方式，是亲手建造其中那些理应存在的部分。" The wager of the mission · 使命的赌注
§ 01.4 · From Mission to Worlds

If this is the why, the rest of the Psyverse is the how. Sixteen worlds unfold the single mission into identities, principles, domains, and the long horizon. 若这是"为何"，心域的其余便是"如何"。十六重世界，将这一条使命展开为身份、原则、领域，与那道漫长的地平线。
