Six Domains六 大 领 域

The six territories where science, technology, and engineering do their deepest work — Matter, Energy, Information, Biology, Consciousness, and Space-Time. 科学、技术与工程深耕于此的六片疆土——物质、能量、信息、生命、意识与时空。

§ 06.1 · A Map of the Territory

Every tool, every theory, every hard-won insight eventually traces back to one of six ground-level territories. They are not disciplines — disciplines are human institutions. These are the actual divisions of the world itself. 每一件工具、每一套理论、每一个来之不易的洞见，最终都追溯至六片基础疆土之一。它们不是学科——学科是人类的建制。这六片，是世界本身的划分。

Choosing a domain is not a career move. It is choosing which face of reality you are willing to press your life against — long enough, hard enough, that it begins to yield its secrets. 选择一个领域，不是职业布局，而是选择你愿意将自己的一生压紧的那一面现实——足够长久，足够用力，直到它开始吐露秘密。

The six are not a hierarchy. They are a topology: each domain borders others, and the most generative ideas live precisely at the edges where two territories meet. 这六者不构成等级，而是一种拓扑：每个领域都与其他领域相邻，而最有生命力的想法，恰恰生长在两片疆土交界的边缘地带。

§ 06.2 · The Six Domains

Territory of Work工作的疆域

i
06 · 01
Matter物 质
The substrate everything else rides on. Atoms, molecules, materials, structures — from quantum mechanics to the yield strength of a bridge. To work in Matter is to negotiate with the deep grammar of the physical world: what holds, what breaks, what can be made to do something new. 一切赖以承载的基底。原子、分子、材料、结构——从量子力学到桥梁的屈服强度。深耕「物质」，是与物理世界的深层语法谈判：什么坚守，什么断裂，什么能被塑造成全新之物。
ii
06 · 02
Energy能 量
The capacity to do work — and therefore the prerequisite for every other domain. Energy is what differentiates a living city from a cold ruin. To understand its flows, conversions, and losses is to understand why civilizations rise or stall. 做功的能力——因而是其他一切领域的前提。能量是区分一座活城与一片冷墟的东西。理解能量的流动、转换与耗散，便是理解文明为何崛起，又为何停滞。
iii
06 · 03
Information信 息
Pattern distinguishable from noise. Shannon proved that information obeys laws as strict as thermodynamics. Every computation, every signal, every model of the world is information being transformed. The 21st century runs, above all, on this domain. 从噪声中可辨识的模式。香农证明，信息遵循与热力学同样严格的定律。每一次计算、每一个信号、每一个关于世界的模型，都是信息在转换。21世纪，首先运行在这片疆域之上。
iv
06 · 04
Biology生 命
Matter organized to persist, replicate, and adapt. Life is the universe's most elaborate information-processing system, encoded in chemistry and selected across deep time. To work in Biology is to read four billion years of solved engineering problems. 被组织起来、能够存续、复制、适应的物质。生命是宇宙最精巧的信息处理系统，编码于化学之中，在漫长的时间里经由选择塑成。深耕「生命」，是在阅读四十亿年已解决的工程难题。
v
06 · 05
Consciousness意 识
The domain that looks back. Consciousness is what happens when biological information-processing becomes aware of itself — and begins to model, question, and steer. Understanding it is not optional: every tool built for humans must eventually answer to it. 会回望自身的领域。意识，是生命的信息处理觉知自身之后所发生的一切——开始建模、追问、导向。理解它不是可选项：一切为人类而造的工具，最终都必须向它交代。
vi
06 · 06
Space-Time时 空
The stage on which all the other domains play out — not a fixed backdrop but a dynamic structure that bends under mass, expands with the universe, and sets the hard limits of causality. To grasp Space-Time is to understand both where we are and what we cannot outrun. 其他一切领域在其上演出的舞台——不是固定的背景，而是在质量下弯曲、随宇宙膨胀、并划定因果律硬边界的动态结构。把握「时空」，是同时理解我们身处何处，以及什么是我们永远无法超越的。
§ 06.3 · Why Six

The logic of the partition划分的逻辑

The choice is not arbitrary, but it is not exhaustive either. These six represent a pragmatic cut: domains broad enough to encompass entire careers and civilizations, yet distinct enough that moving between them feels like crossing a genuine border. 这一划分并非随意，但也非穷尽。这六者是一种务实的切割：每个领域足够宽广，可以容纳整条职业生涯乃至整个文明；又足够分明，使得在它们之间移动，真实地像是越过了某道边界。

Notice what the six domains share: each has a conserved quantity or a fundamental law at its core. Matter has mass and charge. Energy has the laws of thermodynamics. Information has entropy. Biology has natural selection. Consciousness has the hard problem — and Space-Time has the speed of light. Every deep difficulty in science traces back to the tension between these laws. 注意这六个领域的共性：每一个核心都有一个守恒量或基本定律。物质有质量与电荷；能量有热力学定律；信息有熵；生命有自然选择；意识有「难题」；时空有光速。科学中一切深层的困难，都追溯至这些定律之间的张力。

The boundaries are also where the action is. Biochemistry lives between Matter and Biology. Neuroscience lives between Biology and Consciousness. Quantum computing lives between Information and Matter. The most transformative technologies of the coming decades will not respect any single domain's borders. 边界同样是行动发生之处。生物化学活在「物质」与「生命」之间；神经科学活在「生命」与「意识」之间；量子计算活在「信息」与「物质」之间。未来数十年最具变革性的技术，不会遵守任何单一领域的疆界。

§ 06.4 · Depth vs. Breadth

On choosing where to press关于选择在何处施压

A common temptation is to claim all six. The rare, more useful move is to choose one or two — and go deep enough to become genuinely useful at the frontier. 一个常见的诱惑是声称拥有全部六个。更罕见、也更有用的做法，是选择一两个——然后深入到足以在前沿真正有所作为。

Depth is not the same as narrowness. A researcher who has spent ten years in the physics of biological membranes has gone deep into Matter and Biology simultaneously — and can see things no generalist can. The goal is not to pick a box and stay in it; it is to follow a question long enough that it pulls you through several walls. 深度不等于狭窄。一位在生物膜物理学上深耕十年的研究者，是同时深入「物质」与「生命」的——他能看到通才所看不到的东西。目标不是选一个格子然后待在里面；而是追随一个问题足够长久，让它把你带穿几堵墙。

The manifesto does not prescribe which domains to enter. It asks only that the work be real — aimed at something that matters, built on something that is true. 此份宣言不规定要进入哪个领域，只要求工作是真实的——指向真正重要的事物，建立在真正为真的基础之上。

"The universe offers six doors. Behind each is a lifetime of difficulty — and the possibility of understanding something no one has understood before." 「宇宙开了六扇门。每扇门后都有一生的艰难——以及理解从未有人理解过之事的可能。」 On the six domains · 关于六大领域
§ 06.5 · Domains and the Mission

The six domains are where the mission lands. Solving real problems means entering one of these territories, learning its language, and then building something that stands up inside it. The domain is not the mission — it is the terrain the mission is executed across. 六大领域是使命的落地之处。解决真实的问题，意味着进入这些疆土之一，习得它的语言，然后在其中建造出能够屹立的东西。领域不是使命——它是使命展开于其上的地形。
