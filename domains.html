Six Domains六 大 领 域

The six territories where science, technology, and engineering do their deepest work — Matter, Energy, Information, Biology, Consciousness, and Space-Time. 科学、技术与工程深耕于此的六片疆土——物质、能量、信息、生命、意识与时空。

§ 06.1 · A Map of the Territory

Every tool, every theory, every hard-won insight eventually traces back to one of six ground-level territories. They are not disciplines — disciplines are human institutions. These are the actual divisions of the world itself. 每一件工具、每一套理论、每一个来之不易的洞见，最终都追溯至六片基础疆土之一。它们不是学科——学科是人类的建制。这六片，是世界本身的划分。

Choosing a domain is not a career move. It is choosing which face of reality you are willing to press your life against — long enough, hard enough, that it begins to yield its secrets. 选择一个领域，不是职业布局，而是选择你愿意将自己的一生压紧的那一面现实——足够长久，足够用力，直到它开始吐露秘密。

The six are not a hierarchy. They are a topology: each domain borders others, and the most generative ideas live precisely at the edges where two territories meet. 这六者不构成等级，而是一种拓扑：每个领域都与其他领域相邻，而最有生命力的想法，恰恰生长在两片疆土交界的边缘地带。

§ 06.3 · Why Six

The logic of the partition划分的逻辑

The choice is not arbitrary, but it is not exhaustive either. These six represent a pragmatic cut: domains broad enough to encompass entire careers and civilizations, yet distinct enough that moving between them feels like crossing a genuine border. 这一划分并非随意，但也非穷尽。这六者是一种务实的切割：每个领域足够宽广，可以容纳整条职业生涯乃至整个文明；又足够分明，使得在它们之间移动，真实地像是越过了某道边界。

Notice what the six domains share: each has a conserved quantity or a fundamental law at its core. Matter has mass and charge. Energy has the laws of thermodynamics. Information has entropy. Biology has natural selection. Consciousness has the hard problem — and Space-Time has the speed of light. Every deep difficulty in science traces back to the tension between these laws. 注意这六个领域的共性：每一个核心都有一个守恒量或基本定律。物质有质量与电荷；能量有热力学定律；信息有熵；生命有自然选择；意识有「难题」；时空有光速。科学中一切深层的困难，都追溯至这些定律之间的张力。

The boundaries are also where the action is. Biochemistry lives between Matter and Biology. Neuroscience lives between Biology and Consciousness. Quantum computing lives between Information and Matter. The most transformative technologies of the coming decades will not respect any single domain's borders. 边界同样是行动发生之处。生物化学活在「物质」与「生命」之间；神经科学活在「生命」与「意识」之间；量子计算活在「信息」与「物质」之间。未来数十年最具变革性的技术，不会遵守任何单一领域的疆界。

§ 06.4 · Depth vs. Breadth

On choosing where to press关于选择在何处施压

A common temptation is to claim all six. The rare, more useful move is to choose one or two — and go deep enough to become genuinely useful at the frontier. 一个常见的诱惑是声称拥有全部六个。更罕见、也更有用的做法，是选择一两个——然后深入到足以在前沿真正有所作为。

Depth is not the same as narrowness. A researcher who has spent ten years in the physics of biological membranes has gone deep into Matter and Biology simultaneously — and can see things no generalist can. The goal is not to pick a box and stay in it; it is to follow a question long enough that it pulls you through several walls. 深度不等于狭窄。一位在生物膜物理学上深耕十年的研究者，是同时深入「物质」与「生命」的——他能看到通才所看不到的东西。目标不是选一个格子然后待在里面；而是追随一个问题足够长久，让它把你带穿几堵墙。

The manifesto does not prescribe which domains to enter. It asks only that the work be real — aimed at something that matters, built on something that is true. 此份宣言不规定要进入哪个领域，只要求工作是真实的——指向真正重要的事物，建立在真正为真的基础之上。

"The universe offers six doors. Behind each is a lifetime of difficulty — and the possibility of understanding something no one has understood before." 「宇宙开了六扇门。每扇门后都有一生的艰难——以及理解从未有人理解过之事的可能。」 On the six domains · 关于六大领域
§ 06.5 · Domains and the Mission

The six domains are where the mission lands. Solving real problems means entering one of these territories, learning its language, and then building something that stands up inside it. The domain is not the mission — it is the terrain the mission is executed across. 六大领域是使命的落地之处。解决真实的问题，意味着进入这些疆土之一，习得它的语言，然后在其中建造出能够屹立的东西。领域不是使命——它是使命展开于其上的地形。

§ 06.6 · How the Six Domains Interlock

A topology, not a hierarchy拓扑结构，而非层级秩序

The six domains do not stack. They interlock — each one touching at least two others, and every edge between them already a field of its own. 六大领域并非叠加关系，而是彼此咬合——每个领域至少与另外两个相接，而每条边界本身就已是一个独立的研究领域。

Matter × Energy
Thermodynamics & Materials热力学与材料科学
Every material has energy locked in its bonds. Every energy system depends on materials to conduct, contain, or convert it. Batteries, semiconductors, and superconductors all live in this overlap — and every major energy transition in history has first been a materials transition. 每种材料都在化学键中封存着能量；每个能源系统都依赖材料来传导、存储或转换能量。电池、半导体与超导体，皆栖居于这片交叠之地。历史上每一次重大能源转型，本质上首先是一次材料转型。
Information × Matter
Quantum Computing & Lithography量子计算与光刻技术
Information is always physically instantiated. Bits require atoms; qubits require engineered quantum states. The limits of computation are ultimately limits of what matter can be made to hold and transform. Moore's Law was never a law of logic — it was a law of materials engineering. 信息永远以物理形式实例化。比特需要原子；量子比特需要精心构造的量子态。计算的极限，归根结底是物质所能承载与转化之物的极限。摩尔定律从来不是逻辑定律——它是材料工程定律。
Biology × Information
Genomics & Synthetic Biology基因组学与合成生物学
DNA is a four-letter code — a 3.2-billion-character program run by molecular machines. Bioinformatics reads it; synthetic biology rewrites it. The most consequential software of the 21st century may not run on silicon at all. DNA 是四字母编码——一段由分子机器运行的32亿字符程序。生物信息学负责读取，合成生物学负责改写。21世纪影响最深远的软件，或许根本不在硅基上运行。
Biology × Consciousness
Neuroscience & Cognitive Science神经科学与认知科学
Consciousness arises from biology — yet it cannot be reduced to it without remainder. The hard problem lives precisely in this gap. Neuroscience maps the substrate; cognitive science studies the functions; neither alone can explain why there is something it is like to be awake. 意识从生命中升起——却无法在无余项的情况下被还原为生命。「难题」恰好栖居于这一裂隙之中。神经科学描绘底层基底，认知科学研究功能表现，而单凭任一方，都无法解释「何为清醒地活着」这一感受。
Space-Time × Energy
Astrophysics & Cosmology天体物理学与宇宙学
Gravity is geometry — mass curves space-time, and that curvature is what we call energy at the largest scales. Black holes, neutron stars, and the inflationary universe are all experiments in the joint behavior of these two domains, conducted at energies no laboratory will ever reach. 引力即几何——质量弯曲时空，而这种弯曲在最宏观的尺度上就是我们所称的能量。黑洞、中子星与暴胀宇宙，都是在任何实验室永远无法企及的能量条件下，对这两个领域联合行为所做的实验。
Consciousness × Information
Artificial Intelligence & Mind人工智能与心智
If mind is a form of information processing, then intelligence can in principle be substrate-independent. This is the founding hypothesis of AI — and the deepest unresolved question it raises is whether information processing alone is ever enough to produce genuine understanding, or whether something else is always required. 若心智是一种信息处理形式，那么智能在原则上可以与底层基底无关。这是人工智能的立基假说——而它所引发的最深层未决问题是：单凭信息处理，是否真的足以产生真正的理解？抑或始终还需要某种别的东西？

Notice that the most active frontiers of science right now — AI, synthetic biology, quantum computing, neuroscience of consciousness — are all border crossings, not explorations of a single domain's interior. The deepest questions almost always require you to hold two domains at once. 不难发现，当今科学最活跃的前沿——人工智能、合成生物学、量子计算、意识神经科学——无一不是跨越边界的越境之举，而非对单一领域内部的深掘。最深刻的问题，几乎总是要求你同时握住两个领域。

§ 06.7 · Choosing Where to Work

On entering a domain关于进入一片领域

The choice of domain is rarely rational at first. Most people enter because something surprised them — a fact that didn't fit, an experiment that shouldn't have worked, a question that wouldn't leave. 选择领域，最初往往并不理性。大多数人的入口，是某个令他们惊异的时刻——一个不合常理的事实，一个本不该成功的实验，一个挥之不去的问题。

Follow the resistance追随阻力
The right domain is not the one that is easiest to enter. It is the one whose difficulty feels generative rather than merely frustrating — where the obstacles are interesting and the failures teach you something you actually wanted to know. Resistance that instructs is a compass. 正确的领域不是最容易进入的那个，而是那种艰难感觉具有生产性、而非只是令人沮丧的领域——在那里，障碍本身是有趣的，失败教给你的恰好是你真正想知道的事。能够指引方向的阻力，就是罗盘。
Look for the load-bearing question寻找承重问题
Every domain has a few questions that, if answered, would change everything downstream. Identifying these — and honestly asking whether you have the constitution for a decade of work on one of them — is more useful than picking a field from a ranking of career prospects. 每个领域都有少数几个关键问题，一旦得到解答，便会改变其下游的一切。辨别出这些问题——并诚实地自问，你是否具备在其中一个问题上深耕十年的气质——远比从职业前景排行榜上选一个方向有用得多。
Respect the domain's own logic尊重领域自身的逻辑
Every domain has accumulated standards for what counts as evidence, what counts as explanation, and what counts as progress. These standards are not bureaucratic obstacles — they are the accumulated wisdom of every researcher who was wrong in this domain before you. Ignoring them is not independence of mind; it is amnesia. 每个领域都积累了自己的标准：什么算作证据，什么算作解释，什么算作进展。这些标准不是官僚障碍，而是此前在这片领域中出过错的每一位研究者留下的集体智慧。忽视它们，不是思想独立，而是失忆。
Allow for migration允许迁移
Some of the most consequential work happens when someone trained deeply in one domain brings its tools to bear on an apparently unrelated problem in another. The most useful skill is not knowing all of one domain — it is knowing one domain deeply enough to see, with fresh eyes, where its methods might open a stuck problem somewhere else. 最具影响力的工作，有时恰恰发生在这样的时刻：一个在某领域受过深度训练的人，将其工具带入另一个看似无关的问题之中。最有用的能力，不是对某个领域了如指掌，而是对一个领域深入到足以用全新的眼光，看清它的方法在哪里可以打开别处某个僵死的问题。

As AI systems become increasingly capable, the question of what counts as genuine understanding versus sophisticated mimicry becomes legally, ethically, and scientifically urgent. 一切为人类而建的工具，最终都向意识交代——向使用它的感受、它对注意力、记忆与判断力所造成的影响交代。随着人工智能系统的能力日益增强，「什么算作真正的理解，什么只是精巧的模仿」这一问题，在法律、伦理与科学层面都变得愈发紧迫。

The problems it owns它所拥有的难题

The hard problem: why is there subjective experience at all? The neural correlates of consciousness. Building a science of attention and its failures. Understanding the conditions under which AI systems develop something analogous to a point of view — and whether that matters morally. 难问题：为何存在主观体验？意识的神经相关物。建立一门关于注意力及其失效的科学。理解人工智能系统在什么条件下会形成某种类似「视角」的东西——以及这在道德层面是否重要。

Adjacent domains相邻领域

Touches Biology through neuroscience and evolutionary psychology; touches Information through AI and the theory of mind as computation; touches Space-Time in some speculative accounts linking quantum mechanics to consciousness. 通过神经科学与进化心理学与生命相接；通过人工智能与「心智即计算」理论与信息相接；在某些推测性理论中，通过量子力学与意识的可能联系与时空相接。

What it covers涵盖范围

General relativity, cosmology, astrophysics, and the physics of time. Space-Time is not a fixed backdrop but a dynamic structure that bends under mass, expands cosmologically, and sets the hard causality limits that nothing in the universe can exceed. 广义相对论、宇宙学、天体物理学与时间物理学。时空不是固定的背景布景，而是一种动态结构——在质量下弯曲，在宇宙学意义上膨胀，并划定宇宙中任何事物都无法超越的因果律硬边界。

Why it matters为何重要

Understanding Space-Time is understanding the outer structure of everything. The universe's age, its fate, the possibility of other regions causally disconnected from ours — these are not academic questions. They are the frame inside which everything else happens. GPS works only because we understand relativistic time dilation; future interstellar engineering, if it ever comes, will require far deeper mastery. 理解时空，就是理解一切事物的外部结构。宇宙的年龄、它的命运、与我们因果不相连的其他区域存在的可能性——这些都不是学术问题。它们是其他一切事件在其内发生的框架。GPS之所以有效，正是因为我们理解了相对论性时间膨胀；而未来的星际工程，若有朝一日真的到来，将需要更深远的掌握。

The problems it owns它所拥有的难题

The reconciliation of general relativity and quantum mechanics — the deepest open problem in physics. The nature of dark matter and dark energy, which together constitute 95% of the universe's mass-energy. The arrow of time and why entropy increases in only one direction. What, if anything, preceded the Big Bang. 广义相对论与量子力学的调和——物理学中最深层的未解问题。暗物质与暗能量的本质——二者合计构成宇宙质能总量的95%。时间之矢：为何熵只向一个方向增加。大爆炸之前，若有什么的话，究竟是什么。

Adjacent domains相邻领域

Touches Energy through the thermodynamics of black holes and the energy content of the vacuum; touches Matter through quantum gravity and the question of what space-time is made of at the Planck scale; touches Consciousness at the speculative frontier of quantum mind theories. 通过黑洞热力学与真空能量内容与能量相接；通过量子引力与「时空在普朗克尺度上由何构成」这一问题与物质相接；在量子心智理论的推测性前沿与意识相接。
