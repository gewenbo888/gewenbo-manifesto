Three Core Principles三 大 原 则

Truth, Goodness, and Beauty — three standards that have oriented thought and action across every tradition that ever tried to live well. 真、善、美——每一个曾认真尝试好好活着的传统，都以这三个标准校准自己的思想与行动。

§ 03.1 · Why Three

A principle is not a preference. It is a standard you hold even when it costs you — and because it costs you, it carries weight. 原则不是偏好。它是一个即便让你付出代价也要坚守的标准——正因为它有代价，它才有分量。

Most people act from a single implicit principle — maximize gain, minimize loss — and dress it up in the language of the others. These three refuse that compression. They pull in different directions often enough to keep each other honest. 大多数人只遵循一条隐性原则——最大化收益、最小化损失——然后用另外两者的语言加以粉饰。这三条原则拒绝这种压缩。它们彼此的拉力足够频繁，足以相互监督、相互诚实。

Truth keeps you from believing what is comfortable. Goodness keeps you from doing what is merely effective. Beauty keeps you from settling for what merely functions. Together they triangulate a life that can be examined from every direction without flinching. 真，让你不去相信那些只是令你舒适的事。善，让你不去做那些只是奏效的事。美，让你不去接受那些只是能用的东西。三者合力，校准出一种可以从任何角度被审视而不退缩的生命。

§ 03.2 · The Three Principles

真 · 善 · 美Truth · Goodness · Beauty

01 · 真 · TRUTH
Truth · Zhēn真实
What is. The real behind appearances. The ground all thought and action must eventually meet. Truth is not an achievement — it is an obligation: to keep asking, to refuse premature closure, to let evidence update belief even when the update is inconvenient. 实然之物。表象背后的真实。一切思想与行动终须归依的根基。真不是一项成就——它是一种义务：持续追问，拒绝过早封闭，让证据更新信念，哪怕这更新让人不快。
02 · 善 · GOODNESS
Goodness · Shàn善良
What ought to be. Right action toward others, the planet, and the long arc of civilisation. Goodness is not sentiment — it is the willingness to extend your circle of concern beyond what is immediately useful to you, and to hold that extension steady under pressure. 应然之事。对他人、对地球、对文明长河的正当作为。善不是情感——它是一种意愿：将你的关怀圈扩展至对你并非立即有用的地方，并在压力下坚守这种扩展。
03 · 美 · BEAUTY
Beauty · Měi美感
What delights. Form that moves. The harmony of thought, thing, and deed. Beauty is not ornament — it is signal: when a solution, an argument, or a life achieves genuine elegance, that elegance often indicates that something real and right has been found. 悦然之美。动人的形式。思、物、行的和谐。美不是装饰——它是信号：当一个方案、一个论证或一种生命达到真正的优雅，这份优雅往往意味着某种真实而正确的东西已被发现。

Definition定义
Truth is correspondence: a belief or statement that maps accurately onto the way things actually are, independent of what is convenient, comfortable, or socially rewarded. It is not merely factual correctness but the disposition to keep revising your model of reality as evidence changes. 真是对应：一种信念或陈述准确地映射于事物实际所是，而不依赖于什么方便、舒适或受到社会奖励。它不仅是事实上的正确，更是一种随着证据变化而不断修正对现实模型的倾向。
How to test for it如何检验
Ask: would I still hold this belief if it cost me something — status, comfort, a cherished self-image? Can I name the evidence that would change my mind? Am I willing to say this publicly, not just privately? If yes to all three, the belief is likely tracking truth rather than interest. 问自己：如果这个信念让我付出代价——地位、舒适感、珍视的自我形象——我还会持有它吗？我能说出什么证据会改变我的想法吗？我愿意公开说出这一点，而不只是私下认同吗？若三者皆是，这个信念很可能在追踪真实而非利益。
What its absence looks like缺失时的样子
Managed narratives. Beliefs held for tribal solidarity rather than evidence. Arguments that shift to preserve a conclusion. A persistent gap between what is said in public and what is thought in private. Fluency in the language of truth with no genuine exposure to being wrong. 管理过的叙事。为部落团结而持有的信念，而非来自证据。为维护结论而不断转移的论证。公开所说与私下所想之间长期存在的落差。流利地使用真的语言，却从未真正经历过被证伪的体验。
Concrete example具体示例
A researcher who publishes a finding that contradicts their own earlier work — and does so clearly, without hedging — is practicing truth. The act is costly (reputation, embarrassment) and voluntary. That combination is the signature of the principle in action. 一位发表结论与自身早期研究相悖的研究者——并且清晰地呈现，没有模糊处理——正在实践真。这个行动有代价（声誉、难堪），且出于自愿。这种组合，正是这条原则付诸行动的印记。
Definition定义
Goodness is the extension of moral concern beyond the boundary of immediate self-interest. It is not sentiment or warmth alone; it is the structural willingness to bear costs on behalf of those who cannot repay you — strangers, future generations, other species, the systems that sustain life. 善是将道德关怀延伸至直接自身利益边界之外的举动。它不仅仅是情感或温情；而是一种结构性的意愿：愿意为那些无法回报你的人承担代价——陌生人、未来世代、其他物种、维系生命的系统。
How to test for it如何检验
Ask: whose wellbeing am I considering in this decision, and does that list extend beyond people who can benefit me in return? Would I make the same choice if it were anonymous — if no one would ever know? Does the cost I am accepting fall on me, or have I found a way to pass it to someone less visible? 问自己：在这个决定中我考虑了谁的福祉，这份名单是否延伸到了无法回报我的人？如果这是匿名的——没有人会知道——我还会做同样的选择吗？我承受的代价落在我自己身上，还是我找到了一种方式将其转嫁给不那么显眼的人？
What its absence looks like缺失时的样子
Moral concern that stops precisely at the boundary of those who can hurt or help you. Charity performed for visibility. Rules followed to the letter while their spirit is circumvented. Indifference to harms that are real but distant, diffuse, or borne by those without voice. 道德关怀恰好止步于那些能伤害或帮助你的人的边界处。为了被人看见而行善。遵守规则的字面，同时规避其精神。对那些真实但遥远、弥散或由没有话语权的人承担的伤害漠然置之。
Concrete example具体示例
An engineer who flags a safety flaw in a product already approved for launch, knowing it will delay release and draw anger from their team, is practicing goodness. The people protected are users they will never meet. The cost is personal and immediate. The benefit is diffuse and future. 一位工程师在已批准上线的产品中发现安全缺陷并如实上报——明知这会延误发布并引来团队的不满——正在实践善。被保护的人是他们永远不会见到的用户。代价是个人的、即时的。收益是弥散的、未来的。
Definition定义
Beauty is the quality achieved when form, structure, and purpose align so completely that nothing is superfluous and nothing is missing. It is not decoration added after the work is done; it is a property of work done so well that the unnecessary has been stripped away, and what remains has the character of rightness. 美是当形式、结构与目的完全契合——以至于没有任何多余之物，也没有任何缺失——所达到的品质。它不是工作完成后再添加的装饰；而是工作做得足够好的属性，多余之物已被剥去，剩下的具有一种「正确」的特质。
How to test for it如何检验
Ask: could anything be removed without loss? Does the form serve the function, or is the form concealing a weak function? Does the thing look different from every angle, and is that difference consistent or arbitrary? Does encountering it produce a sense of inevitability — the feeling that it could not have been otherwise? 问自己：是否可以去掉什么而不带来损失？形式是在服务功能，还是在掩盖一个薄弱的功能？从不同角度看这个东西有何不同，这种差异是一致的还是任意的？接触它是否产生一种必然感——觉得它不可能是另一种样子？
What its absence looks like缺失时的样子
Ugliness that goes unnoticed because the threshold for acceptable has been set to merely functional. Prose that conveys information but produces no image. Interfaces that work but create friction. Arguments that are technically valid but laborious to follow. The sense that something is finished but not done. 丑陋无人察觉，因为「可接受」的门槛已被设定为「仅能运作」。传递信息但不产生任何意象的文字。功能正常但制造摩擦的界面。技术上有效但令人费力追踪的论证。某件事感觉已经完成，但尚未「做好」的感觉。
Concrete example具体示例
Euler's identity — eiπ + 1 = 0 — unites five fundamental constants in a single equation with no spare parts. Mathematicians describe it as beautiful not as a compliment but as a diagnosis: the elegance signals that something structurally deep has been captured, not merely derived. 欧拉恒等式——eiπ + 1 = 0——将五个基本常数统一在一个没有任何多余部分的等式中。数学家称其为美丽，不是恭维，而是一种诊断：这份优雅表明某种结构上的深层之物已被捕捉，而非仅仅被推导出来。
§ 03.3 · Truth — The First Obligation

Seek what is real, not what is reassuring追求真实，而非安慰

Truth is easy to endorse in the abstract. In practice it asks something specific: a willingness to be wrong, publicly, and to update without retreating into defensiveness. Most drift toward managed narratives — versions of events that place themselves in the best light. Truth resists that drift. 真在抽象层面容易获得认同。在实践中它要求某种具体的事：愿意在公开场合出错，并在更新时不退回到防御性姿态。大多数人倾向于管理叙事——将自己置于最佳位置的事件版本。真，抵制这种偏移。

This is not scepticism as posture. It is the disciplined practice of holding models loosely — using them fully while remaining genuinely ready to revise them. Science is the institutional embodiment of this practice. But the practice is older and broader than any institution. 这不是作为姿态的怀疑主义。而是一种有纪律的实践：松散地持有模型——充分使用它们，同时真正准备好修正它们。科学是这一实践的制度化体现。但这一实践比任何制度都更古老、更宽广。

At the personal scale: truth means saying what you actually think, not what the room expects. It means tracking your beliefs to their sources and noticing when a source has dried up. It means distinguishing between what you know and what you want to believe — and keeping that distinction visible. 在个人层面：真意味着说出你真正所想，而非房间期待的话。它意味着将你的信念追溯到其来源，并在来源枯竭时察觉。它意味着区分你所知与你希望相信的——并让这一区别始终可见。

The world does not care what you believe about it. Truth is the record of that indifference — and our best tool against it. 世界不在乎你对它有何信念。真，是那份冷漠的记录——也是我们对抗它的最好工具。 On the first principle · 第一原则注
§ 03.4 · Goodness — The Widening Circle

Extend the range of what you are responsible for扩展你负有责任的范围

Goodness is measured by the radius of your concern. An infant is concerned only with its own body. A child adds family. Adulthood, in the moral sense, is the ongoing expansion of that radius — to strangers, to future people, to other species, to the systems that sustain all of the above. 善由你关怀的半径来衡量。婴儿只关心自己的身体。孩子加入了家人。道德意义上的成熟，是那个半径持续扩展的过程——扩展到陌生人、未来的人、其他物种、以及维系上述一切的系统。

Civilisation has been, in large part, the slow institutional encoding of this expansion: property rights protect strangers from each other; constitutions protect future generations from present ones; environmental law protects species from the side-effects of our commerce. Each encoding is imperfect. The direction is legible. 文明在很大程度上就是这一扩展被缓慢制度化编码的过程：产权保护陌生人彼此不受侵犯；宪法保护未来世代不受当代人的任意处置；环境法保护物种免遭我们商业活动的副作用。每一次编码都不完美。但方向是清晰可辨的。

At the personal scale: goodness means choosing the harder option when it benefits someone outside yourself. It means building things that outlast your own interest in them. It means noticing when your gain is someone else's cost — and letting that noticing change the calculation. 在个人层面：善意味着当更困难的选择让自身之外的人受益时，选择它。它意味着建造那些比你自身的利益活得更久的东西。它意味着察觉你的收益何时是他人的代价——并让这种察觉改变你的盘算。

§ 03.5 · Beauty — The Signal in the Form

Elegance is not a luxury — it is a compass优雅不是奢侈品——它是罗盘

Beauty is the most misunderstood of the three principles. It is often treated as decoration — something added after the real work is done. The opposite is closer to true. When a derivation, a design, or a piece of prose achieves genuine elegance, it usually means the underlying structure has been understood deeply enough that all the unnecessary has been stripped away. 美是三条原则中最易被误解的一条。它常被当作装饰——在真正的工作完成之后才添加的东西。与之相反的说法更接近真相。当一个推导、一个设计或一段文字达到真正的优雅，通常意味着其底层结构已被理解得足够深，以至于所有多余之物都已剥去。

This is why mathematicians trust beautiful proofs more than ugly ones — not out of aesthetics, but because elegance has historically been a reliable indicator that a proof captures something structurally true rather than just technically valid. The same holds in engineering: a bridge that looks wrong usually fails, because structural coherence and visual coherence track each other. 这就是为什么数学家比起丑陋的证明更信任美丽的证明——不是出于审美，而是因为优雅在历史上一直是可靠的指示器，表明一个证明捕捉到了某种结构上的真实，而非仅仅在技术上有效。在工程中也是如此：一座看上去不对劲的桥往往会倒塌，因为结构上的连贯性与视觉上的连贯性相互追踪。

At the personal scale: beauty means caring about form — in speech, in making, in the pace and texture of a day. It means refusing to treat craft as expendable. It means attending to the quality of experience, not only its outcomes, because how something is done is part of what it is. 在个人层面：美意味着关心形式——在言语中、在制作中、在一天的节奏与质地中。它意味着拒绝将工艺视为可抛弃之物。它意味着关注体验的质量，而不仅仅是其结果，因为事情的完成方式是其本质的一部分。

When truth, goodness, and beauty align in a single act — that is the signature of a life well made. 当真、善、美在一个行动中合而为一——那便是一个精心铸就的生命的印记。 On the trinitas · 论三位一体
§ 03.6a · When the Three Conflict

Truth vs Kindness vs Elegance真与善、美之间的冲突

The three principles are not always allies. Each can, in context, demand something the other two forbid. These conflicts are not edge cases — they are daily life. Knowing how each collision tends to unfold is the beginning of adjudicating it well. 三条原则并非总是盟友。在特定情境下，每一条都可能要求另外两条所禁止的事。这些冲突不是边角情形——它们就是日常生活。了解每种碰撞通常如何展开，是妥善裁决它的起点。

Truth × Goodness真 × 善
The honest wound
The true thing and the kind thing are not the same thing often enough to treat them as automatically aligned. A doctor who gives a patient accurate prognosis data that the patient cannot yet process; a friend whose honest assessment of a plan arrives before the person has the resources to act on it — truth here is technically correct and temporarily cruel. 真实之事与善意之举足够频繁地不是同一回事，无法将二者视为自然一致。一位医生向尚无法消化的患者提供准确的预后数据；一位朋友在对方尚无资源付诸行动之前就给出了对计划的诚实评价——在这里，真在技术上是正确的，在暂时意义上是残酷的。
Adjudication: truth earns priority when withholding it would produce a worse outcome than delivering it. The test is whether the person can act on the truth, not whether they will be comfortable receiving it. Timing and form are Goodness's contribution; the content belongs to Truth. 裁决：当隐瞒真相比传递它会产生更糟糕的结果时，真获得优先权。判断标准是当事人能否对真相采取行动，而非他们接受时是否会感到舒适。时机与方式是善的贡献；内容属于真。
Goodness × Beauty善 × 美
The useful and the well-made
A crudely built shelter that houses people in a crisis is good. A beautifully designed shelter that takes three times as long to build and houses fewer people is beautiful. When resources are scarce and time is short, Goodness and Beauty pull in different directions: more people helped sooner, or a form that does not insult the people it helps. 一个粗糙建造的避难所在危机中庇护了人们，它是善的。一个设计精美但需要三倍时间建造且容纳人数更少的避难所，它是美的。当资源匮乏、时间紧迫，善与美向不同方向拉扯：更快帮助更多人，还是一种不侮辱被帮助者的形式。
Adjudication: when stakes are life-and-death urgent, Goodness leads. When they are not, the choice for ugly-but-effective is often a lazy default rather than a genuine trade-off. Most decisions in ordinary life have enough slack to accommodate both; the conflict is real primarily at extremes. 裁决：当赌注是生死攸关的紧急情况时，善领先。当不是这种情况时，选择「丑但有效」往往是懒惰的默认选项，而非真正的权衡取舍。日常生活中的大多数决策有足够的余地同时容纳二者；这种冲突主要在极端情况下才是真实的。
Beauty × Truth美 × 真
The elegant wrong answer
A beautiful theory, a clean narrative, a satisfying explanation — all of these have a pull that can survive the evidence against them. Keats thought beauty and truth were identical. They are not. Phlogiston was an elegant theory. The steady-state universe was a beautiful model. Elegance is a useful prior, not a proof. 一个美丽的理论、一个干净的叙事、一个令人满足的解释——所有这些都有一种吸引力，能够在反驳证据面前存活。济慈认为美与真是同一的。它们不是。燃素说是一个优雅的理论。稳态宇宙是一个美丽的模型。优雅是一个有用的先验，而非证明。
Adjudication: when empirical evidence and elegant theory diverge, truth wins. The appropriate response to a beautiful-but-wrong theory is to mourn it briefly and then revise. The mourning is appropriate; the revision is mandatory. 裁决：当经验证据与优雅理论相悖时，真获胜。对于一个美丽但错误的理论，适当的回应是短暂地惋惜，然后修正。惋惜是恰当的；修正是必须的。
§ 03.6b · How to Adjudicate

Working rules for genuine conflicts面对真实冲突时的运作规则

No algorithm resolves every collision between the three principles. But certain heuristics have proven robust across many contexts. They do not eliminate difficulty; they make it navigable. 没有任何算法能解决三条原则之间的每一次碰撞。但某些启发式规则已在许多情境下被证明是稳健的。它们不能消除困难；它们使困难变得可以驾驭。

Situation情境 Working rule运作规则
High stakes, irreversible 高风险、不可逆 Truth first. The cost of acting on a false belief under irreversible conditions exceeds the social cost of delivering the truth. Do not defer truth to preserve comfort when you cannot later undo the decision. 真优先。在不可逆条件下基于错误信念行动的代价，超过传递真相的社会代价。当你事后无法撤销决定时，不要为了维护舒适而推迟真相。
Scarce resources, competing needs 资源稀缺、需求竞争 Goodness sets the floor. Before optimising for elegance or accuracy, ask who is not being served. Efficiency gains that come at the price of excluding the most vulnerable are not net gains under this framework. 善设定底线。在为优雅或精确性优化之前，先问谁没有被服务到。以排斥最脆弱者为代价换来的效率提升，在这一框架下不是净收益。
Elegant theory vs awkward data 优雅理论 vs 尴尬数据 Data wins, but the loss is worth noting. When evidence defeats a beautiful model, the appropriate register is mild grief — the theory was doing real cognitive work and its loss is real. Pretending otherwise is not truth, it is indifference dressed as rationality. 数据获胜，但损失值得记录。当证据打败一个美丽的模型时，恰当的回应是轻微的惋惜——这个理论曾做过真正的认知工作，其损失是真实的。假装不是这样，不是真，而是用理性面具掩盖的冷漠。
Truth that harms the vulnerable 伤害脆弱者的真相 Deliver truth, but choose its form with care. Goodness does not license concealment; it licenses attention to timing, framing, and the recipient's capacity to receive. The goal is a true thing that lands — not a true thing deployed as a weapon, and not a false thing deployed as comfort. 传递真相，但谨慎选择其形式。善不授权隐瞒；它授权关注时机、措辞框架和接受者的承受能力。目标是一个能落地的真实之物——而非被当作武器的真实之物，也非被当作安慰的虚假之物。
All three can coexist 三者可以共存 Most of the time, the conflict is not as stark as it appears. A message can be true, kind in its framing, and clear in its form simultaneously. The discipline is to look for that solution before accepting that a genuine trade-off is required — because genuine trade-offs are rarer than they seem. 大多数时候，冲突并不像看上去那么尖锐。一条信息可以同时是真实的、措辞上善意的、形式上清晰的。这里的纪律是在接受真正的权衡取舍是必要的之前，先寻找那个解决方案——因为真正的权衡取舍比看上去的更罕见。
§ 03.6 · The Tension and the Unity

Three pulls, one direction三股张力，一个方向

The three principles are not always in harmony. Truth can be brutal; Goodness can ask for silence; Beauty can be achieved through illusion. The friction between them is not a flaw in the framework — it is the framework. 三条原则并不总是和谐共处。真可以是残酷的；善有时要求沉默；美可以经由幻觉来实现。它们之间的摩擦不是这个框架的缺陷——它就是这个框架本身。

A life lived under only one principle becomes a caricature: pure Truth-seeking can become cold and cruel; pure Goodness can become sentimental and naive; pure Beauty-seeking can become self-indulgent and disconnected. The discipline is to hold all three in tension simultaneously — to let each check the excesses of the others. 只在一条原则下生活的人生会变成一幅漫画：纯粹追求真会变得冷漠而残酷；纯粹追求善会变得多愁而天真；纯粹追求美会变得自我放纵而脱离现实。这里的纪律是同时保持三者的张力——让每一条原则约束其他两条的过度之处。

When all three converge — when an act is honest, beneficial, and well-made — the result has a quality that cannot be produced by chasing any one of them alone. That convergence is rare. Recognising it when it happens, and working toward it when it does not, is what the principles are for. 当三者汇聚——当一个行动诚实、有益、且精心完成——其结果拥有一种单独追求其中任何一条都无法产生的品质。这种汇聚是罕见的。在它出现时认出它，在它尚未出现时朝它努力——这便是这三条原则存在的意义。

These three principles sit inside the broader Psyverse — downstream of the One Mission (use science, technology, and engineering to solve real problems), upstream of every domain, identity, and vision that follows. They do not specify what to do. They specify the standard by which whatever is done ought to be judged. 这三条原则存在于更广阔的心域之中——位于一使命（用科学、技术和工程解决真实问题）的下游，位于此后每一个领域、身份与愿景的上游。它们不规定做什么。它们规定凡所做之事应以何标准来判断。
