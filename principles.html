Three Core Principles三 大 原 则

Truth, Goodness, and Beauty — three standards that have oriented thought and action across every tradition that ever tried to live well. 真、善、美——每一个曾认真尝试好好活着的传统，都以这三个标准校准自己的思想与行动。

§ 03.1 · Why Three

A principle is not a preference. It is a standard you hold even when it costs you — and because it costs you, it carries weight. 原则不是偏好。它是一个即便让你付出代价也要坚守的标准——正因为它有代价，它才有分量。

Most people act from a single implicit principle — maximize gain, minimize loss — and dress it up in the language of the others. These three refuse that compression. They pull in different directions often enough to keep each other honest. 大多数人只遵循一条隐性原则——最大化收益、最小化损失——然后用另外两者的语言加以粉饰。这三条原则拒绝这种压缩。它们彼此的拉力足够频繁，足以相互监督、相互诚实。

Truth keeps you from believing what is comfortable. Goodness keeps you from doing what is merely effective. Beauty keeps you from settling for what merely functions. Together they triangulate a life that can be examined from every direction without flinching. 真，让你不去相信那些只是令你舒适的事。善，让你不去做那些只是奏效的事。美，让你不去接受那些只是能用的东西。三者合力，校准出一种可以从任何角度被审视而不退缩的生命。

§ 03.2 · The Three Principles

真 · 善 · 美Truth · Goodness · Beauty

01 · 真 · TRUTH
Truth · Zhēn真实
What is. The real behind appearances. The ground all thought and action must eventually meet. Truth is not an achievement — it is an obligation: to keep asking, to refuse premature closure, to let evidence update belief even when the update is inconvenient. 实然之物。表象背后的真实。一切思想与行动终须归依的根基。真不是一项成就——它是一种义务：持续追问，拒绝过早封闭，让证据更新信念，哪怕这更新让人不快。
02 · 善 · GOODNESS
Goodness · Shàn善良
What ought to be. Right action toward others, the planet, and the long arc of civilisation. Goodness is not sentiment — it is the willingness to extend your circle of concern beyond what is immediately useful to you, and to hold that extension steady under pressure. 应然之事。对他人、对地球、对文明长河的正当作为。善不是情感——它是一种意愿：将你的关怀圈扩展至对你并非立即有用的地方，并在压力下坚守这种扩展。
03 · 美 · BEAUTY
Beauty · Měi美感
What delights. Form that moves. The harmony of thought, thing, and deed. Beauty is not ornament — it is signal: when a solution, an argument, or a life achieves genuine elegance, that elegance often indicates that something real and right has been found. 悦然之美。动人的形式。思、物、行的和谐。美不是装饰——它是信号：当一个方案、一个论证或一种生命达到真正的优雅，这份优雅往往意味着某种真实而正确的东西已被发现。
§ 03.3 · Truth — The First Obligation

Seek what is real, not what is reassuring追求真实，而非安慰

Truth is easy to endorse in the abstract. In practice it asks something specific: a willingness to be wrong, publicly, and to update without retreating into defensiveness. Most drift toward managed narratives — versions of events that place themselves in the best light. Truth resists that drift. 真在抽象层面容易获得认同。在实践中它要求某种具体的事：愿意在公开场合出错，并在更新时不退回到防御性姿态。大多数人倾向于管理叙事——将自己置于最佳位置的事件版本。真，抵制这种偏移。

This is not scepticism as posture. It is the disciplined practice of holding models loosely — using them fully while remaining genuinely ready to revise them. Science is the institutional embodiment of this practice. But the practice is older and broader than any institution. 这不是作为姿态的怀疑主义。而是一种有纪律的实践：松散地持有模型——充分使用它们，同时真正准备好修正它们。科学是这一实践的制度化体现。但这一实践比任何制度都更古老、更宽广。

At the personal scale: truth means saying what you actually think, not what the room expects. It means tracking your beliefs to their sources and noticing when a source has dried up. It means distinguishing between what you know and what you want to believe — and keeping that distinction visible. 在个人层面：真意味着说出你真正所想，而非房间期待的话。它意味着将你的信念追溯到其来源，并在来源枯竭时察觉。它意味着区分你所知与你希望相信的——并让这一区别始终可见。

The world does not care what you believe about it. Truth is the record of that indifference — and our best tool against it. 世界不在乎你对它有何信念。真，是那份冷漠的记录——也是我们对抗它的最好工具。 On the first principle · 第一原则注
§ 03.4 · Goodness — The Widening Circle

Extend the range of what you are responsible for扩展你负有责任的范围

Goodness is measured by the radius of your concern. An infant is concerned only with its own body. A child adds family. Adulthood, in the moral sense, is the ongoing expansion of that radius — to strangers, to future people, to other species, to the systems that sustain all of the above. 善由你关怀的半径来衡量。婴儿只关心自己的身体。孩子加入了家人。道德意义上的成熟，是那个半径持续扩展的过程——扩展到陌生人、未来的人、其他物种、以及维系上述一切的系统。

Civilisation has been, in large part, the slow institutional encoding of this expansion: property rights protect strangers from each other; constitutions protect future generations from present ones; environmental law protects species from the side-effects of our commerce. Each encoding is imperfect. The direction is legible. 文明在很大程度上就是这一扩展被缓慢制度化编码的过程：产权保护陌生人彼此不受侵犯；宪法保护未来世代不受当代人的任意处置；环境法保护物种免遭我们商业活动的副作用。每一次编码都不完美。但方向是清晰可辨的。

At the personal scale: goodness means choosing the harder option when it benefits someone outside yourself. It means building things that outlast your own interest in them. It means noticing when your gain is someone else's cost — and letting that noticing change the calculation. 在个人层面：善意味着当更困难的选择让自身之外的人受益时，选择它。它意味着建造那些比你自身的利益活得更久的东西。它意味着察觉你的收益何时是他人的代价——并让这种察觉改变你的盘算。

§ 03.5 · Beauty — The Signal in the Form

Elegance is not a luxury — it is a compass优雅不是奢侈品——它是罗盘

Beauty is the most misunderstood of the three principles. It is often treated as decoration — something added after the real work is done. The opposite is closer to true. When a derivation, a design, or a piece of prose achieves genuine elegance, it usually means the underlying structure has been understood deeply enough that all the unnecessary has been stripped away. 美是三条原则中最易被误解的一条。它常被当作装饰——在真正的工作完成之后才添加的东西。与之相反的说法更接近真相。当一个推导、一个设计或一段文字达到真正的优雅，通常意味着其底层结构已被理解得足够深，以至于所有多余之物都已剥去。

This is why mathematicians trust beautiful proofs more than ugly ones — not out of aesthetics, but because elegance has historically been a reliable indicator that a proof captures something structurally true rather than just technically valid. The same holds in engineering: a bridge that looks wrong usually fails, because structural coherence and visual coherence track each other. 这就是为什么数学家比起丑陋的证明更信任美丽的证明——不是出于审美，而是因为优雅在历史上一直是可靠的指示器，表明一个证明捕捉到了某种结构上的真实，而非仅仅在技术上有效。在工程中也是如此：一座看上去不对劲的桥往往会倒塌，因为结构上的连贯性与视觉上的连贯性相互追踪。

At the personal scale: beauty means caring about form — in speech, in making, in the pace and texture of a day. It means refusing to treat craft as expendable. It means attending to the quality of experience, not only its outcomes, because how something is done is part of what it is. 在个人层面：美意味着关心形式——在言语中、在制作中、在一天的节奏与质地中。它意味着拒绝将工艺视为可抛弃之物。它意味着关注体验的质量，而不仅仅是其结果，因为事情的完成方式是其本质的一部分。

When truth, goodness, and beauty align in a single act — that is the signature of a life well made. 当真、善、美在一个行动中合而为一——那便是一个精心铸就的生命的印记。 On the trinitas · 论三位一体
§ 03.6 · The Tension and the Unity

Three pulls, one direction三股张力，一个方向

The three principles are not always in harmony. Truth can be brutal; Goodness can ask for silence; Beauty can be achieved through illusion. The friction between them is not a flaw in the framework — it is the framework. 三条原则并不总是和谐共处。真可以是残酷的；善有时要求沉默；美可以经由幻觉来实现。它们之间的摩擦不是这个框架的缺陷——它就是这个框架本身。

A life lived under only one principle becomes a caricature: pure Truth-seeking can become cold and cruel; pure Goodness can become sentimental and naive; pure Beauty-seeking can become self-indulgent and disconnected. The discipline is to hold all three in tension simultaneously — to let each check the excesses of the others. 只在一条原则下生活的人生会变成一幅漫画：纯粹追求真会变得冷漠而残酷；纯粹追求善会变得多愁而天真；纯粹追求美会变得自我放纵而脱离现实。这里的纪律是同时保持三者的张力——让每一条原则约束其他两条的过度之处。

When all three converge — when an act is honest, beneficial, and well-made — the result has a quality that cannot be produced by chasing any one of them alone. That convergence is rare. Recognising it when it happens, and working toward it when it does not, is what the principles are for. 当三者汇聚——当一个行动诚实、有益、且精心完成——其结果拥有一种单独追求其中任何一条都无法产生的品质。这种汇聚是罕见的。在它出现时认出它，在它尚未出现时朝它努力——这便是这三条原则存在的意义。

These three principles sit inside the broader Psyverse — downstream of the One Mission (use science, technology, and engineering to solve real problems), upstream of every domain, identity, and vision that follows. They do not specify what to do. They specify the standard by which whatever is done ought to be judged. 这三条原则存在于更广阔的心域之中——位于一使命（用科学、技术和工程解决真实问题）的下游，位于此后每一个领域、身份与愿景的上游。它们不规定做什么。它们规定凡所做之事应以何标准来判断。
